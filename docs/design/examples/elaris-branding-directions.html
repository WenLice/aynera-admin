How to show Elaris — and five modern website directions

Based on your product docs: Elaris is a verified relationship journey for India (Delhi & Bangalore first) — curated Meet, mutual Focus, enforced Together, and a private Couple Space. The brand must feel intentional, not swipe-app.

What the name needs to signal

Elaris should read as a calm, premium relationship brand — closer to Hinge’s quiet confidence than to Tinder energy or Shaadi brochure tone.

  • Promise: When you choose each other, we stop the search.
  • Tagline: Meet intentionally. Focus mutually. Grow privately.
  • Hero rule from docs: brand name is hero-level, not a tiny nav logo.
“This isn’t another dating app — I get what happens when we choose each other.”

Practical recommendation (still just guidance)

  1. Monogram + caps (this trial): Best default surface is plum fill (rose-gold E on plum) or paper (plum E + dark caps). Use the header row lockup in the nav; scale the same system large in the hero.
  2. Hero: The monogram dusk trial above works if you want product-mark energy with your current colors. For quieter premium, still compare Direction 2.
  3. Always: Keep ELARIS large in the first viewport when using caps — don’t rely on the small E mark alone.
  4. Avoid: Tiny logo-only branding, purple-tech gradients, matrimony red/gold clichés, swipe-card hero grids.
