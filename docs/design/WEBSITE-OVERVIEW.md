# Aynera Public Website — Design Overview v1

**Audience:** Gen Z + young professionals (≈22–35), Delhi and Bangalore  
**Goal:** Make people feel “this is different from Tinder/Bumble” in 3 seconds, then apply for the waitlist.  
**Stack:** Next.js App Router + TypeScript + CSS modules / Tailwind

---

## What the best sites teach us

| Reference | What they do well | What Aynera takes | What we avoid |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Hinge** (hinge.co) | One sharp promise (“designed to be deleted”); editorial calm; outcomes over addiction | Outcome-led brand line; quiet confidence; journey narrative | Love-scientist jargon; US-only tone |
| **Bumble** | Safety as a visible product; optimistic energy | Safety centre as a first-class page; trust without fear mongering | Yellow clone; women-message-first as the whole brand |
| **Aisle** | India-first intentional dating; curation over volume | Cohort / waitlist honesty; serious intent without matrimony vibes | Looking like Shaadi.com; caste-first framing |
| **Gen Z brand sites (2026)** | Instant value prop; bold type; real photos; purposeful motion; authenticity | Expressive typography; full-bleed imagery; micro-motion; mobile-first | Maximal neon chaos; AI-smooth stock; endless scroll fluff |

---

## Positioning on the web

**Not:** another swipe marketplace landing page.  
**Not:** a matrimony brochure.  
**Yes:** a relationship journey brand — *when you choose each other, we stop the search.*

**Tone:** Warm, modern, confident, India-aware, bilingual-ready later. Speaks to Gen Z without trying to “act Gen Z.”

---

## Visual direction

| Element | Choice | Why |
|---------|--------|-----|
| **Brand colour** | Deep teal `#1F6B5C` + ink `#0F1C1A` | Trust + commitment; distinct from Tinder red / Bumble yellow |
| **Accent** | Soft coral `#E07A5F` | Human warmth for CTAs — used sparingly |
| **Surfaces** | Soft stone / off-white with grain texture | Atmosphere without flat white or purple gradients |
| **Type** | Display: **Syne** (expressive) · Body: **Outfit** | Gen Z–readable, not Inter/Roboto; brand-forward headlines |
| **Imagery** | Full-bleed candid couple / city moments (Unsplash, curated) | Real visual anchor — café, evening walk, conversation — not stock handshake love |
| **Motion** | 2–3 intentional moves: hero fade/rise, journey step reveal, CTA hover | Presence, not noise |

## Site map (website-first)

1. **Home** — Brand hero + promise + journey teaser + apply CTA  
2. **How it works** — Verify → Introductions → Focus → Together  
3. **Apply** — Delhi and Bangalore waitlist form  
4. **Safety** — Tools, reporting, offline concern  
5. **Legal** — Privacy, Terms, Account deletion, Grievance  

---

## Home page composition (first viewport)

Per brand rules — **one composition**, not a dashboard:

1. **Aynera** (hero-level brand)  
2. **One headline:** When you choose each other, we stop the search.  
3. **One line:** Verified introductions for people who want something real — then a private space when you both choose.  
4. **One CTA group:** Apply for early access · How it works  
5. **One dominant full-bleed image** (edge-to-edge atmosphere)

No stats strip, no floating badges on the hero image, no card grid in the first viewport.

## Gen Z checklist for this site

- [x] Value clear in under 3 seconds  
- [x] Brand name is the hero, not a tiny logo  
- [x] Real photography, not plastic romance stock  
- [x] Short copy; no corporate fluff  
- [x] Mobile-first layout and thumb CTAs  
- [x] Inclusive language (not matrimony-only, not casual-only)  
- [x] Safety visible without making the brand feel scary  
- [x] Motion that feels intentional

## Success for the website

The site succeeds if a 24-year-old and a 33-year-old both say:  
*“This isn’t another dating app — I get what happens when we choose each other.”*  
…and they apply for Delhi or Bangalore early access.
