## How it works (WEB-02)

**Step 1 — Verify**  
Real people only. Phone, email, and video selfie before you meet anyone.

**Step 2 — Meet intentionally**  
2–3 curated introductions per week — with clear reasons, not endless swiping.

**Step 3 — Focus together**  
Mutually pause new matches while you explore one connection.

**Step 4 — Just us**  
When you both confirm exclusivity, discovery stops and your private couple space opens.

## Mobile — Onboarding

**OTP screen:**  
We'll send a code to verify your number. Standard SMS rates may apply.

**Age gate:**  
ElAris is for adults 18+. Your date of birth is used for age assurance and is not shown on your public profile.

**Liveness:**  
A short video selfie helps us confirm you're real. Raw verification media is deleted per our retention policy.

**Pending approval:**  
Your profile is with our team. Most reviews complete within 48 hours. You won't appear in discovery until approved.

## Focus Mode

**Request title:** Focus on each other?  
**Body:**  
For the time you choose, both of you disappear from new introductions. Unrelated chats move to archive. Either person can end Focus anytime.

**Duration options:** 14 days · 30 days · 60 days

**Partner view:**  
[S Name] would like to Focus with you for 30 days. New introductions would pause for both of you.

**Decline (private):** No shared rejection message is sent.

---

## Together Mode

**Request title:** Choose each other exclusively?  
**Consequences (must show before confirm):**
- Both profiles removed from all discovery
- New matches and likes disabled on ElAris
- Previous conversations archived privately
- Your couple space opens

**Confirm copy:**  
I understand and want to continue — requires independent confirmation from both.

**Exit notice (partner):**  
Together Mode has ended.

## Verified badge explainer

**Title:** What verified means  
**Body:**  
Phone verified, video selfie passed, and age assurance completed. Verification helps trust — it does not guarantee behaviour. Report concerns anytime.
